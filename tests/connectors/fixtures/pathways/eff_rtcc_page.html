Neighborhood Watch Out: Cops Are Incorporating Private Cameras Into Their Real-Time Crime Centers

Police departments are partnering with the vendor Fusus to fold privately owned camera feeds — doorbell and business cameras enrolled through Neighborhood Watch-style registration programmes — into a real-time crime center feed. This page is a policy explainer, not a deployment report: it describes what the technology is and what the rules should be.

An integration hub lets an RTCC ingest feeds from thousands of private cameras and push alerts to officers. Whether any particular centre is operating is a separate question this page does not answer.
