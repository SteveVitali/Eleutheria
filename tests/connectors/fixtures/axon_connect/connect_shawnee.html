Connect Shawnee County and the City of Topeka

Connect Shawnee County and the City of Topeka is a new public safety program enabling the people of Shawnee County and the City of Topeka to help keep their community safe.

LEVEL 1: Register Your Cameras

Let's work together to fight crime! Register your security cameras with Connect Shawnee County and the City of Topeka today to help identify area cameras in case of an incident.

Camera registration takes less than one minute via our secure online portal. Registering your cameras does not allow law enforcement investigators access to your live video stream - it only enables investigators to know a camera is present at your location and easily request video evidence should an incident occur.

LEVEL 2: Integrate Your Cameras

Businesses and residents can take community security one step further by giving law enforcement investigators direct access to your camera feed in case of a nearby emergency. All you need is a small CORE device that you plug into your camera system.

Both the Shawnee County Sheriff's Office and Axon Fusus policies require the camera owner to give explicit permission before any sharing.

Only authorized law enforcement investigator users have access to the camera registry map in this jurisdiction.
