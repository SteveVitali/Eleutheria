City of Columbia, Missouri — Police Department
2025 Annual Surveillance Technology Report

Date: March 15, 2026

Pursuant to the City of Columbia Law Enforcement Surveillance Oversight
Ordinance, the Columbia Police Department is required to document and
publicly report its use of surveillance technology. The Columbia City Council
enacted the ordinance establishing annual public reporting.

Surveillance Technology: License Plate Readers
Surveillance Technology: CCTV

License plate readers capture plate images at fixed locations; the CCTV
network covers downtown intersections. Each system is reported at the
department level; no per-vehicle or per-person fields are published.

Retention: plate data retained for up to 90 days.
Sharing: data shared with the Missouri State Highway Patrol.
