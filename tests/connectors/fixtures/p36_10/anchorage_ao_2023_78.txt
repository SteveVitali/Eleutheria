MUNICIPALITY OF ANCHORAGE
ASSEMBLY ORDINANCE No. AO 2023-78

AN ORDINANCE OF THE ANCHORAGE MUNICIPAL ASSEMBLY AMENDING ANCHORAGE
MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER 3.102, MUNICIPAL USE OF SURVEILLANCE TECHNOLOGIES,
TO ADD A REQUIREMENT FOR A BODY-WORN CAMERA POLICY

For reading: July 11, 2023
Date: July 11, 2023

THE ANCHORAGE ASSEMBLY ORDAINS:

Section 1. Anchorage Municipal Code chapter 3.102, Municipal Use of
Surveillance Technologies, is hereby amended to add a requirement that the
Anchorage Police Department adopt a body-worn camera policy as part of its
surveillance technology reporting under the chapter.

Section 2. This ordinance recites AO 2020-116(S), the ballot proposition
establishing the municipal use of surveillance technologies chapter.

Technology: Body-Worn Cameras
