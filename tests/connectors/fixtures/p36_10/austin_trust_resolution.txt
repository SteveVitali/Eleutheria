City of Austin — City Council
Resolution — File ID 26-1072

Date: February 5, 2026

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF AUSTIN:

The City Council adopts the Transparent and Responsible Use of
Surveillance Technology (TRUST) Act framework and directs the City Manager
to return to Council not later than March 24, 2026, with an ordinance
amending the City Code to regulate the adoption, acquisition, deployment,
use, and review of surveillance technology by any City department.
