Regular Meeting of Municipal Council

Council meetings are open to members of the public, who are welcome to speak at the designated public comment times.  Note: The council may hold a pre-meeting caucus one-half hour prior to any scheduled council meeting.

 

When: Second and fourth Wednesdays at 6 p.m.

Where: Council Chambers, City Hall

Contact: City Clerk's Office

Caucus Meeting of the Municipal Council

The purpose of the caucus meeting is to discuss agenda items for the upcoming regular council meeting.  Caucuses are open to the public, however, there is no opportunity for the public to speak.  The Council may also hold closed caucuses during which the public is excluded.  In addition, the council may hold a pre-meeting caucus one-half hour prior to any scheduled council meeting.

 

When:  Typically at 4:00 p.m. on the Monday immediately before the Wednesday Regular Meeting

Where: Efrain Rosario Memorial Caucus Room, unless otherwise designated

Agenda: Available on the Digital Agenda portal under "Schedule of Meetings"
