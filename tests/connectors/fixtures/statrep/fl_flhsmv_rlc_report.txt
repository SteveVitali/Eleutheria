Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles
Red Light Camera Program Analysis - Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Summary

Date: December 1, 2025
Technology: traffic infraction detectors

Under s. 316.0083(4), F.S., the department submits this annual analysis of
red light camera programs (traffic infraction detectors) to the Florida
Legislature, compiled from the jurisdictions' survey responses.

Violations issued statewide: 210,445
Jurisdictions reporting a red light camera program: 54

All figures are program-level aggregates compiled from the jurisdictions'
annual survey responses; no driver, plate, or per-violation records appear
in this summary.
