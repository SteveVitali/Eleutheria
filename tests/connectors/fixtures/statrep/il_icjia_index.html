Drone Surveillance Reports

Under the Freedom From Drone Surveillance Act (725 ILCS 167), enacted by the Illinois General Assembly, the Illinois Criminal Justice Information Authority annually publishes a report listing every law enforcement agency that owns a drone and the number of drones owned.
