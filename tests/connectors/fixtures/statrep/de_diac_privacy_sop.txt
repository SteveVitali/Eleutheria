Delaware Information and Analysis Center
Privacy, Civil Rights, and Civil Liberties Protection Policy

Date: March 1, 2023
Technology: fusion center

The Delaware Information and Analysis Center (DIAC) maintains this privacy
standard operating procedure under 28 CFR Part 23 and section 511 of the
Implementing Recommendations of the 9/11 Commission Act of 2007, as required
of fusion centers receiving federal funds.

The policy governs the collection, use, retention, and dissemination of
information by the Delaware Information and Analysis Center, including
access controls, audit logging, training, and the annual privacy-policy
review conducted by the DIAC privacy officer with oversight-board approval.

This is a governance instrument; it reports no operational use, and no
individual records appear in this publication.
