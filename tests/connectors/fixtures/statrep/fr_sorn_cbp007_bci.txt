Federal Register Document 2016-01331 (81 FR 4040)
Privacy Act of 1974; Department of Homeland Security/U.S. Customs and Border
Protection-007 Border Crossing Information (BCI) System of Records

Publication Date: 01/25/2016
System identifier: DHS/CBP-007
Technology: license plate readers

In accordance with the Privacy Act of 1974, 5 U.S.C. 552a, the Department of
Homeland Security proposes to update and reissue the DHS/CBP-007 Border
Crossing Information system of records. The notice names the system's data
categories verbatim; this publication is the statutory notice of the records
system and publishes no individual records.
