Maryland Department of State Police
Annual Report on Cell-Site Simulator Use

Date: January 31, 2025
Technology: cell-site simulator

Under Chapters 222 and 223 of the Acts of 2020 (Maryland General Assembly),
the Department of State Police submits this annual report on the use of
cell-site simulator technology for the preceding calendar year.

Cell-site simulator deployments during the reporting period: 4
Exigent circumstances deployments: 1

All figures are institutional aggregates; the report publishes no target
identifiers, case names, or individual records.
