ARVADA POLICE DEPARTMENT
Facial Recognition Accountability Report — Calendar Year 2025
Published under C.R.S. 24-18-301 through 24-18-309 (SB22-113), enacted by
the Colorado General Assembly
Date: January 15, 2026
Technology: facial recognition system

Use statistics (calendar year 2025):
97 of 13,396 investigations used facial recognition; 44 returned a positive
match and 53 returned no match.

Provider access:
The department accesses Lumen through the Colorado Information Sharing
Consortium; the department does not maintain its own facial recognition
system.
