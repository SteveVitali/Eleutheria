Virginia State Crime Commission
Date: January 21, 2026
Technology: ALPR

Under Code of Virginia § 2.2-5517, the Virginia State Police approved eight
ALPR systems for statewide use. Participating agencies subscribe to Vigilant
Solutions and Thomson Reuters CLEAR plate-data services.
