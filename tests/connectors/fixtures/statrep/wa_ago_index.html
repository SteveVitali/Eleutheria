Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) System Registration

Under RCW 10.130, enacted by the Washington State Legislature as ESSB 6002 (2021), an agency that operates or intends to operate an ALPR system must register with the Attorney General's Office. The Office publishes the registrations below as filed; the Office has not verified the contents of any registration.
