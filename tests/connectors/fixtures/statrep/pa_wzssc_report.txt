Pennsylvania Department of Transportation
Work Zone Speed Safety Camera Program - Annual Report

Date: April 2, 2026
Technology: work zone speed safety cameras

The Pennsylvania Department of Transportation, jointly with the Pennsylvania
Turnpike Commission and the Pennsylvania State Police, submits this report
under 75 Pa.C.S. 3369(h)(4) to the Pennsylvania General Assembly.

Violations issued statewide during the program year: 112,530
Notices mailed: 98,112

The report is the statute's program-level aggregate; it publishes no
vehicle, owner, or per-violation record.
