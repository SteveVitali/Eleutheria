ANNUAL REPORT ON UNMANNED AERIAL VEHICLE USE
Minnesota Department of Public Safety, Bureau of Criminal Apprehension
Prepared for the Minnesota Legislature under Minn. Stat. 626.19
Date: June 15, 2025
Technology: unmanned aerial vehicle

In 2025, Minnesota law enforcement agencies reported a total of 9,080 UAV
uses in circumstances where judicial authorization was not required, up
from 6,603 reported in 2024.

Reported uses by agency (calendar year 2025):
• Hennepin County Sheriff's Office — 312 UAV uses
• Minnesota State Patrol — 280 UAV uses
• Duluth Police Department — 141 UAV uses
