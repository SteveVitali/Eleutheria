ANNUAL REPORT OF THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY
ON MAINE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES' USE OF UNMANNED AERIAL VEHICLES
Submitted to the Joint Standing Committee on Judiciary
Date: December 15, 2016
Technology: unmanned aerial vehicle

Under 25 MRSA 4501, enacted by the Maine Legislature, the department reports
each calendar year on law enforcement use of unmanned aerial vehicles.

UAV uses reported statewide: 0
To the knowledge of the department, no law enforcement agency in the State
of Maine has used an unmanned aerial vehicle in the reporting period.
