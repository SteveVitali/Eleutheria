Federal Register Document 2012-12396
Privacy Act of 1974; U.S. Customs and Border Protection, DHS/CBP-006
Automated Targeting System, System of Records

Publication Date: 05/22/2012
System identifier: DHS/CBP-006
Technology: automated targeting system

In accordance with the Privacy Act of 1974, 5 U.S.C. 552a, the Department of
Homeland Security updates and reissues the DHS/CBP-006 Automated Targeting
System system of records notice. The notice is the statutory publication of
the records system; it publishes no individual records.
