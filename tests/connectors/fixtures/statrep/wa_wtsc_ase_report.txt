Washington Traffic Safety Commission
Automated Safety Enforcement - Annual Report to the Washington State Legislature

Date: January 15, 2026
Technology: automated traffic safety cameras

Pursuant to RCW 46.63.220(6)(b)(ii), the Washington Traffic Safety Commission
compiled this statewide report for the transportation committees of the
Washington State Legislature. Cities and counties operating automated traffic
safety cameras reported aggregate outcomes for the 2025 calendar year under
RCW 46.63.220(6)(b)(i).

Infractions issued: 41,903
Municipal reports received: 29

All figures are program-level aggregates; the report publishes no vehicle
owner, plate, or trip-level information.
