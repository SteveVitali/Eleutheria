VIRGINIA STATE POLICE
ALPR REPORTING REQUIREMENTS — VERSION 2

CJIS Data Analysis & Reporting Team
Date: March 3, 2026
Technology: ALPR

Under Code of Virginia § 2.2-5517(J), each agency operating an approved
ALPR system reports the following metrics to the Virginia State Police.

CAMERAS OWNED OR LEASED BY TYPE
The reporting form enumerates fixed, mobile, and portable camera units by
type and by system.

QUERIES CONDUCTED
The form records every query of the ALPR database by calendar month and by
agency.

STOPS INITIATED
Stops initiated by notification reason are recorded in aggregate.
