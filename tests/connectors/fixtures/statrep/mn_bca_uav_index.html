Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) Annual Reports

Under Minn. Stat. 626.19, Minnesota law enforcement agencies report each unmanned aerial vehicle use to the Bureau of Criminal Apprehension; the BCA files the statewide annual report with the Minnesota Legislature by June 15 each year.
