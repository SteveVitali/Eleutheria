Date: October 14, 2022
Technology: commercial telemetry data

CBP Commercial Telemetry Data Evaluation

Under Section 208 of the E-Government Act of 2002 (44 U.S.C. 3501 note), the
Department of Homeland Security publishes this privacy impact assessment for
the U.S. Customs and Border Protection commercial telemetry data evaluation
pilot. The assessment describes the program, the categories of information
purchased from commercial vendors, and the privacy mitigations applied.

This publication is the program-level assessment; it contains no individual
records and reports no operational counts.
