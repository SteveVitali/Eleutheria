Facial Recognition Technology

Colorado law (CRS 24-18-301 through 24-18-309, enacted by the Colorado General Assembly as SB22-113) requires state and local agencies that use a facial recognition system to publish an accountability report on the agency's public website.

In 2025, 97 of 13,396 investigations used facial recognition; 44 returned a positive match and 53 returned no match. The department accesses Lumen through the Colorado Information Sharing Consortium.
