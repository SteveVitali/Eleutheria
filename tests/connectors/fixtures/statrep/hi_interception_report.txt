Supreme Court of the State of Hawai'i - Administrative Director of the Courts
Judiciary Annual Interception Report

Date: January 15, 2025
Technology: interception of wire, oral, or electronic communications

Report to the Hawaii State Legislature pursuant to Hawaii Revised Statutes
section 803-47(b) (HRS 803-47).

For calendar year 2024 there were no applications for orders authorizing or
approving the interception of wire, oral, or electronic communications in the
State of Hawai'i. No interceptions were authorized, executed, or reported.

Appendix A of the statutory filing lists judge and prosecutor names; those
appendix fields are institution-internal and are not published data of this
report.
