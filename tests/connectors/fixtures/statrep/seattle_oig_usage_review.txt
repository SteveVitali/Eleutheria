Office of Inspector General - City of Seattle
Surveillance Usage Review - Seattle Police Department

Date: June 30, 2025
Technology: acoustic gunshot detection

This annual usage review is submitted under SMC 14.18 (the Seattle
surveillance ordinance enacted by the Seattle City Council). The OIG reviewed
the Seattle Police Department's use of the reviewed technology during the
review period.

Uses in the review period: 37

The review examines frequency and patterns of use, data sharing, security,
and civil-liberties impacts at the program level. It publishes no officer
names, no per-search records, and no individual case data.
