APD LICENSE PLATE READER AUDIT — MAY 2025

City of Austin Office of the City Auditor
Date: May 15, 2025
Technology: ALPR

This audit report reviewed APD's ALPR trial deployed March 2024 through
March 2025. The ALPR system in use operated fixed cameras supplied by Flock
Safety under a trial agreement. The audit found that APD Risk Management
conducts quarterly audits of system access.
