WATECH — FACIAL RECOGNITION ACCOUNTABILITY REPORT
Filed with the Technology Services Board under RCW 43.386, enacted by the
Washington State Legislature
Date: August 28, 2025
Technology: facial recognition

This accountability report covers the Washington Technology Solutions
facial recognition service pilot filed under the agency's Notice of Intent
(08-28-2023). It describes the service's purpose, the data it operates on,
and the accountability measures in place for the pilot period.

Provider: the pilot operates on the agency's hosted service.
