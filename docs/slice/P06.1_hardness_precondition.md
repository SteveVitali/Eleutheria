# P06.1 — Hardness precondition (pre-registered, declared BEFORE the slice)

**Ticket:** `docs/tickets/P06.1__vertical-slice.md` · **Phase 6** — vertical slice
(HARD SYNCHRONIZATION GATE, §54 / SIG-ENG-034).

This document is the ticket's *pre-registration*. Per the ticket and §51.1, the
hardness precondition MUST be declared **before** the slice begins, so that a
too-easy jurisdiction cannot be rationalised after the fact. It replaces the
unfalsifiable "a slice that surfaced no problems was too easy" with a **checkable
property of the chosen jurisdiction, fixed in advance**. The four properties are
asserted programmatically in `tests/acceptance/test_hardness_precondition.py`
(deterministic); the *judgement* that the jurisdiction and its contradictions are
genuine is the agentic part recorded here.

> **A jurisdiction failing ANY of the four properties below MUST NOT be used.**

## Chosen slice jurisdiction

**Oklahoma City, Oklahoma — the Oklahoma City Police Department (OKCPD) Flock
Safety ALPR program** (Master Agreement **C241032**, ~$270,000/yr, in force since
2023; renewed by City Council on **2026-08-18**).

Selection rationale: it is one of the most heavily and independently documented
municipal ALPR deployments in the United States as of the slice date
(2026-09-01), with a live procurement record, a contested council renewal, an
active community field-mapping effort, sustained investigative reporting, an
agency policy document, and a state statute bearing on use — i.e. the whole depth
J-1 (§2.2, Appendix D) demands, on real data.

### Method note (honesty about what the slice is)

Phase 6 is deliberately sequenced **before** the records/parsing connectors (P07),
the reconciliation engine (P08), the portal layer (P11), and the public API/web
surfaces (P14/P15). There is therefore no live connector that can fetch a
transparency portal, a contract PDF, or a council packet yet. The slice's
evidence is committed **fixtures that faithfully transcribe cited public
sources** (URLs recorded as each artifact's `stable_locator`), captured as of
2026-09-01. Figures are transcribed from the public reporting cited below, not
fetched by a live connector. This is called out again in the retrospective.

## The four checkable properties, and the OKC data satisfying each

### 1. ≥ 3 independent source families — **SATISFIED (6)**

| # | Source family | Reliability | Example artifact (stable_locator) |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Executed contract / procurement record | R1 | Master Agreement C241032; renewal letters (City of OKC procurement) |
| F2 | Municipal governance record | R2 | City Manager renewal memo (agenda 2026-08-04); Council vote 2026-08-18 (5–3) |
| F3 | Community field-mapping | R5 | DeFlock / DeFlockOKC crowdsourced camera map (~299) — the OSM connector family |
| F4 | Investigative journalism | R4 | Journal Record, The Oklahoman, KGOU/KOSU, News9 |
| F5 | Agency policy | R2 | OKCPD Operations Manual §5-118 (ALPR policy) |
| F6 | Statutory / legal regime | R1 | Oklahoma statute 47 O.S. §7-606.1 |

### 2. ≥ 2 claims on one predicate that disagree — **SATISFIED**

`claimed_device_count` (total ALPR cameras associated with Oklahoma City) carries
two independently-sourced, dated claims that disagree:

- **~299** — DeFlock community map, widely cited ("the community map shows 299";
  "an estimated 300 ALPR cameras in the OKC metro area").
- **~190** — Chief Ron Bacy, 2026-08-18: *"OKCPD has 90 cameras, while businesses
  own around 100 within city limits."* (90 + ~100 ≈ 190).

They disagree (299 vs 190) and the disagreement is genuine — a community/metro
count vs an official city-limits count. SIG keeps **both** visible (no collapse),
emits a `value_disagreement` contradiction, and generates a research task.

*(Distinct from the count-basis conflation used to demonstrate
`PREDICATE_CONFLATION`: contracted 90 vs mapped 299 — three answers to three
questions, per §29.1 / Appendix D.)*

### 3. ≥ 1 asset with no operator — **SATISFIED**

DeFlock has mapped ALPR devices in OKC whose operator is not confirmed — the
~100 business/HOA-hosted and otherwise unattributed poles beyond the 90
police-operated cameras. At least one mapped `physical_asset` in the slice graph
carries no `operator`, exercising the §29.2 attribution gap (SC-08.1).

### 4. ≥ 1 lifecycle transition evidenced by a dated document — **SATISFIED**

The **2026-08-18** City Council renewal is a dated, documented procurement
lifecycle transition (`active → renewed`): a one-year extension through
2027-06-30, adding an amendment giving the City (not Flock) sole power to share
data with federal agencies and reducing data retention from 30 to 7 days.
Evidenced by the council record and multiple outlets.

## Genuine contradictions the slice will surface (judgement, agentic)

- **`policy_configuration_divergence`** — OKCPD Operations Manual §5-118 states
  ALPR data *"will not be shared as part of a law enforcement information
  database,"* while the configured Flock network made OKC data pullable by 109
  local agencies (a nationwide sharing platform), pre-renewal. Rendered without
  collapse.
- **`value_disagreement`** — the 299-vs-190 claimed-count disagreement above.
- **Retention divergence** — 30-day (Flock default / prior manual) vs 7-day
  (post-renewal amendment): configured vs policy-written vs vendor-default
  retention, each with its own evidence.
- **`predicate_conflation`** — fired on the deliberate conflation of the
  contracted (90) and mapped (299) counts, proving the §29.1 guard.

## Pre-registration statement

The jurisdiction and the four properties above were fixed **before** any slice
code, fixtures, reconciliation, or dossier were written. The commit that
introduces the slice introduces this file alongside it; the properties are not
revised to fit whatever the implementation happened to produce.
