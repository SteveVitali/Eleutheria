## Goal
The `records` connectors (MuckRock, NextRequest, DocumentCloud) and the `RecordsRequest` entity:
public-records requests cited as provenance and their released documents captured, with
`no_responsive_records` treated as a positive coverage finding and the rate-limited APIs used as
targeted lookups only — never crawled.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **`records` connector** on the P04.1 framework, writing `RecordsRequest` entities, `EvidenceArtifact` rows, and released-document captures — SIG-INGEST (records).
2. **MuckRock as api_v2** with auth on all data endpoints and a short-lived JWT (the outline's api_v1 reference is wrong) — SIG-INGEST (records).
3. **`RecordsRequest` entity** with its predicate surface (`requesting_party`, `target_agency`, `response_status`, `statutory_basis`, `platform`, `external_id`, `released_documents`, …) — SIG-ONTO (RecordsRequest).
4. **`no_responsive_records` → CoverageRecord**: `response_status = 'no_responsive_records'` is a positive finding feeding the `NO_EVIDENCE_FOUND` coverage model, not a null result — SIG-ONTO-040.
5. **Targeted-lookup discipline**: rate-limited APIs are queried as targeted lookups for known requests/agencies; no crawl is attempted (§26 rate-limit + prefer-offered-channel rules) — SIG-INGEST-036.
