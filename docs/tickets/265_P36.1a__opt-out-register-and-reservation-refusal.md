## In scope (deliverables)

1. **Host-level opt-out register** — a committed, machine-readable register consulted *before every fetch* (rule 7's "immediately"); a listed host is refused at the gate, no rebuild/redeploy needed to apply an entry.
2. **Reservation detection + refusal** — affirmative machine-readable reservations are detected and refused at the ingestion gate:
   - `Content-Signal` directives (e.g. `ai-train=no`);
   - TDM-reservation signals;
   - EU DSM Article 4 reservations.
   `policy/crawler.py`'s parser is wired into the real path (it is currently test-only); refusal is recorded on the source's rights record.
3. **Distinct state** — *affirmatively refused* is stored distinctly from `UNDETERMINED`: the first closes the Stage-0 conversation, the second invites it (SIG-INGEST-046c; a reservation refusal MUST NOT be stored as, or collapse into, `UNDETERMINED`).
4. **GL-GATE-08 preserved, verbatim** — robots disallows remain disregarded under the ratified posture (ADR-168; A-5) and are *disclosed* — fetched hosts carry `robots_disregarded` provenance with host+count inventory. A robots disallow is **not** itself an affirmative rights reservation: `disallowed`/`unretrievable` verdicts continue to proceed and are stamped; only an affirmative reservation refuses. No counsel-step re-gating, no posture change.
5. **Fixture proof** — a committed fixture demonstrates a reservation is refused by the ingestion gate (and a robots-disallow-only fixture demonstrates it still proceeds, stamped `robots_disregarded`, disclosed).
6. **S6/S6c carries** — any SIG-INGEST-046c case encountered is routed to the operator (S6R-28: into the GATE-G5 packet); classified licence metadata of A-8 express-terms rows carrying an affirmative reservation goes back to the operator (S6 R-19).
7. Tests under `tests/connectors`/`tests/policy`: register consulted pre-fetch, reservation refusal distinct from `UNDETERMINED`, Content-Signal/TDM/Art-4 fixtures, robots-disallow ≠ reservation, no `ingestion_permitted` mutation.
8. Requirement stamps in the PR body: `SIG-INGEST-046c` (and the rule-7/§26 requirement id if the spec assigns one).
