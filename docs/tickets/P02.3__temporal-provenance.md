# P02.3 — Temporal semantics and provenance

- **Sequence:** 8 of 46
- **Phase:** 2 — The bitemporal claim and evidence spine
- **base_branch:** current checkout — the branch the previous ticket left checked out (stacked PR chain); `main` for P00.1
- **Depends on (must land earlier in the stack):** P02.1 (the claim columns/hooks), P02.2 (the `ingest_run` lineage record)
- **Run:** `implement-spec spec=docs/tickets/P02.3__temporal-provenance.md`

## Goal
Make time and provenance queryable: EDTF encoding with a pinned deterministic envelope derivation, the two
as-of query axes, the eight temporal invariants as enforced checks, and PROV-O export over the ingest-run
lineage — so a citation is reproducible and imprecise dates stay imprecise.

## Load
- `docs/2_canonical_design_spec.md` §9 (temporal semantics: five dimensions, kinds, as-of, absence,
  invariants), §10 (epistemic model — the evidence→claim chain and confidence fields), §16.7 (EDTF storage
  encoding), §20 (ontology/vocabulary/schema versioning).
- Part 0, Part I §3, Appendix C (DDL), Appendix E glossary. Lineage anchors: §21.6 (PROV-O), §17.7 (`ingest_run`).

## In scope — deliverables
1. **EDTF Level 1 encoding** (`valid_edtf`, `observed_edtf`, `published_at`) with a **pinned, versioned,
   deterministic** envelope-derivation function whose widening rules are recorded in `ruleset_version` — SIG-STORE-021, SIG-TIME-006.
2. **The `kind` discriminators** on every bound; `valid_to_kind = 'ongoing'` never means "true now" and is
   rendered with its observation date — SIG-TIME-004/005.
3. **As-of query functions** over the two queryable axes (`as_of_world`, `as_of_belief`) with explicit
   defaults, incl. the fourth (belief-pinned) form that makes a past citation reproducible — SIG-TIME-007/008/009.
4. **The four absence states** (`NOT_RESEARCHED` / `NO_EVIDENCE_FOUND` / `EVIDENCE_OF_ABSENCE` / `UNRESOLVED`)
   distinguishably encoded — SIG-TIME-010/011/012.
5. **Temporal invariants TI-1…TI-8** as DB constraints or run-failing data-quality checks, with property tests — SIG-TIME-013/014.
6. **PROV-O export** over the P02.2 `ingest_run` lineage (captures/claims = Entity; runs/extractions =
   Activity; connectors/curators/sources = Agent; `revises_claim` = `prov:wasRevisionOf`) — SIG-INGEST-015/016.

## Out of scope (do NOT do here)
- The physical claim/resolution DDL, append-only enforcement, RLS — P02.1 (this ticket fills the temporal
  columns/hooks it laid).
- The OCFL store, capture pipeline, and the `ingest_run` recording itself — P02.2.
- Converting an observation into a validity interval — that is a **resolution-layer** operation (P08.1, §28).
