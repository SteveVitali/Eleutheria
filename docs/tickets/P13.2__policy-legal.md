## Goal
Model what governs the infrastructure and surface where it is disobeyed: the `Policy` and
`LegalInstrument` entities, policy-versus-configuration divergence rendered as a first-class finding
(never editorially collapsed), and the ability to hold a curated source index *as an index* without
normalizing its entries into claims.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **`Policy`** as an identity-only entity with its §11.13 predicate surface (policy_type; polymorphic repeatable `applies_to` Organization/Deployment/Product; adopting_body; enforcement_mechanism) — SIG-ONTO-034.
2. **`LegalInstrument`** ([NEW], §11.14) with instrument_type, enacting_body, jurisdiction, citation, effective/sunset dates, and `constrains_*` / `requires_authorization_of` edges — so an *arrêté préfectoral*, a CNIL decision, or a CCOPS approval requirement has somewhere to live.
3. **Policy/configuration divergence as a rendered finding**: SIG detects and surfaces the divergence with **both sides' evidence** and MUST NOT editorially collapse it; the canonical instance (a written policy prohibiting immigration-related use alongside an enabled immigration hotlist) is expressible and renderable — SIG-RECON-044. `Policy` MUST NOT be merged with `ConfigurationState` — SIG-ONTO-034.
4. **Curated index held without normalization**: SIG can hold a curated source index *as an index* without normalizing its entries into claims — SIG-EPIS-030.
