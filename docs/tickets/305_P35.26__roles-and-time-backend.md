## Goal

Make the graph's who-does-what edges truthful in role and time. CP-7's change: (a) **operator vs publisher separated** — the edge an emitter writes names the role it actually evidences (a source that *publishes* a dataset does not thereby *operate* the cameras it lists — SIG-TRUST-003: publisher/host/operator/owner/vendor stay distinct unless explicitly evidenced); the EFF and OSM connectors' conflation is the recorded defect this row fixes at the emitter and at the schema; (b) **dated edges** — role/relation edges carry valid time (`valid_from`/`valid_to` or an explicit `undated` marker) so a role claim asserts *when* it held; (c) **no 1970 placeholders** — an edge with no evidenced date is `undated`, never epoch-zero (the placeholder that made every undated edge look 1970-asserted dies); (d) **supersession** — when a later claim evidences a role change, the supersession is a recorded edge succession (the old edge's `valid_to` set by correction claim, per the append-only model — never an in-place edit); (e) **explicit unknown** — a missing operator is a first-class `operator_unknown`/`not_asserted` state (SIG-ONTO-028), never a guessed publisher-as-operator fallback. The EFF + OSM re-ingest under the corrected emitters and the edge rematerialize are the live legs; the ODbL/OSM compartment's licence separation is untouched (the OSM re-ingest stays inside its compartment).

## Interfaces and collision surface

- The role/relation edge schema (`db/`) — edges gain `role` (closed vocabulary: operator, publisher, host, owner, vendor, maintainer, …), `valid_from`/`valid_to` or `undated`, and the supersession link; additive migration.
- The EFF emitter — its dataset-publisher claim stops minting `operates`/`operated_by` edges; it emits `published_by`/`listed_by` with the evidence role it actually has.
- The OSM emitter — same separation (OSM's mapper/registry data is evidence about listed features, not operation evidence); the re-ingest stays inside the ODbL compartment — compartment-scoped claims only, never crossing the licence boundary into the public compartment's role graph without the compartment's own export rules.
- `reconcile/materialize.py` — the edge materialization rebuilds edges under the new schema; bounded, resumable, idempotent; appends corrected materialization.
- The supersession path — a role-change claim sets the prior edge's `valid_to` via correction claim (the append-only succession); no `UPDATE`.
- P35.27's resolver truth (downstream) — the dated edges + separated roles are what let its tolerance-aware dissent read *when* a role held; the S-edge orders this row before it.
