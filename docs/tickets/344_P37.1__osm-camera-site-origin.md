## Goal

Make `osm_overpass` an **origin** of camera-site claims, not a re-publisher of mirror registries (I8 NEW-1: I7 treated this as configuration; it is an M-sized connector and ER change). Land, code only: (1) the camera-site predicate family emitted from OSM elements carrying `surveillance:type` or `highway=speed_camera` — `camera_external_ref = osm:<type>/<id>`, `camera_latitude/longitude` (way centre), `camera_coordinate_source=osm`, `camera_jurisdiction = sig.unresolved` until PKG-06b, `camera_operator` from `operator=` (organisation only), and a `technology` claim through P35.15b's machinery (`alpr-fixed`, the P36.3 ATE subtype where it applies); (2) the mapper-identity stop — `out body center qt;`, never `out meta` (SIG-INGEST-045e: no `user`/`uid` anywhere), with edit detection moved to the existing content-digest snapshot diff and the pinned test updated in the same PR; (3) national and global tiling — `out count;` per US-state area then every non-zero country, `out body center qt;` per non-empty area, 180 s timeout, ≈30 s between queries, ≈70–90 queries/run, ContentDrift against the discovery counts; (4) the RF-derived screen — `source`/`survey` tags naming Wi-Fi/BLE/RF detection keep 3-decimal-place coarsened coordinates until the SIG-PUB-011–014 residential screen lands (a publication-side dependency, never an ingest blocker); (5) the subjects join the camera-site ER pool — `_TARGETS_SQL` extended or a pool-admitted prefix, or both; (6) fixtures + the shadow-diff test — an OKC slice and a US-state slice (≈200 ALPR nodes) whose shadow diff against the republish fixture shows ≥95 % 1g/3g merges (shared OSM id or ≤1 m) in a test ER run. No live fetch, no mirror retirement, no rematerialize — those are P37.2's legs.
