5. **Reporter status (C4 NEW-17; DR-C4-12).** The reporter status view shows the outcome kind, the approved public response and the correction or release link once published (SIG-FIND-008; SIG-GOV-011's counts are later).
6. **Proposal shape (C4 NEW-18) and form fixes (C4 NEW-19).** Proposal shape validated at proposal time; category placeholder, viewport meta, and a record-page "report this" deep link that stays unlinked while the receiver is not operating.
