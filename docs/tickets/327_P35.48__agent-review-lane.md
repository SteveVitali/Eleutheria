## Goal

Stand up the **agent review lane** exactly as L3 §4.3 draws it — labelled, blind, never gating: a **seeded weekly stratified sample** (the L2 C14 sampler: 50 sites + 30 edges across the named strata — new sources, possible duplicates, sites in the largest dossier deltas, operator edges, procurement edges, plus a fixed simple-random block), each item labelled by **two fresh agent contexts** with different prompts, neither seeing the other's answer, the matcher's tier or any earlier label, both records stored; **disagreement routes the item to the maintainer queue stratum** (the queue exists; the OPCHECK protocol itself was dropped at B-31 — recorded); agreement is recorded as **"agent consensus", never correct**, and the agent–agent κ publishes as "agreement between two AI runs", never accuracy (same-family agreement is correlated — the Q-L3-4 = no answer). SIG-CONF-002's segregation binds by construction and by test.
