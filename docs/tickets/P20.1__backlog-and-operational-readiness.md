2. **`docs/build/BACKLOG.md`** — the same items grouped by landing ticket, each with a one-line rationale; a "P22+ unscheduled" section for anything no P21 ticket owns (this is allowed; it must be *recorded*, not guessed into a ticket).
3. **`docs/build/BACKLOG_THEMES.md`** — ≤10 themes (e.g. "persistence of the annotation layer", "live wiring", "rights & compact", "publication surfaces", "infra & cost", "spec drift", "docs hygiene") each listing its bl_ids and the ticket that retires most of it.
4. **`docs/build/tools/check_backlog.py`** (stdlib): parses `docs/risk_register.md` (rows under the deferred-class headings) and `docs/adr/*.md` (`## Revisit trigger`) and `LEDGER_DEFERRALS.md` (LD ids) and asserts each appears in exactly one `sources` cell of `BACKLOG.csv`; asserts enum validity; prints counts (`risk deferred rows: N/N mapped; ADR revisit triggers: M/M; LD rows: 90/90`).
5. **Risk register cross-refs only:** append ` → BL-nnn` to the end of the first cell of each mapped `RISK-*` row (no other edit to the register); ADR files untouched (the backlog references them, not vice versa — ledger §4.3).
6. **`docs/build/OPERATIONAL_READINESS.md`** — (a) capability rows: ingest (per connector: 9 connectors), resolve, reconcile, tasks, API, exports, web, contribution-back, international adapters; columns: code state (from the matrix), rights state (from `sig-connectors validate`: permitted/UNDETERMINED/loadable counts per connector's sources), infra needed, human gate (HG-nn), first target (OKC or n/a), owning ticket; (b) **minimum viable live slice** for OKC = the exact source list, the connectors, the tables, the pages; (c) **critical path** = DECISION_MEMO §6 re-verified against the current matrix/closure — every step has a ticket and a proof command; **no TBD**; (d) the human-gate checklist HG-01..14 with "what unblocks it" and "who" (role, not name); (e) cost posture: zero-cost mode (SIG-STORE-003, SIG-GOV-021) vs the egress risk (RISK-P0-07, ADR-015) — what exists (nothing deployed; no Dockerfile/compose — SCOPING §(vi)) and what P21.4/P21.5 add.
