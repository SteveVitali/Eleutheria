## Goal

Every camera-registry record carries a declared technology — today every one of the ~232,628 camera subjects is emitted `record_kind="traffic_camera"` with no technology predicate (F-329: "the public cannot tell ALPR from traffic cameras"; F-523: 77.4% of them are ALPR, police CCTV or enforcement cameras *by their own registry target*). This row lands the code half of PKG-07: a `technology` slug per `camera_registry_targets.toml` / `dot_511_targets.toml` target, `dot_511.py` emitting it as a claim plus the subject subtype, the export column, and the dossier/map facets that consume it. The slug is declared on the target, never inferred from source-name substrings (that substring guard is exactly the NEW-7/FM-05 defect this replaces). The ~200k-subject append-only backfill is **P35.15b**, not here.

## In scope (deliverables)

1. **Per-target `technology` slug.** Every `camera_registry_targets.toml` and `dot_511_targets.toml` target carries a `technology` value that is a member of the generated technology registry; a missing or out-of-registry slug fails validation (`sig-connectors validate` / the target-schema check) — the default is never silently `traffic_camera`.
2. **Emission.** `dot_511.py` emits the technology as a claim on the subject (`technology` predicate, registered through the ontology) plus the subject's subtype/record-kind from the target declaration — an ALPR target's subjects are `alpr-*`, a CCTV target's are the camera family, and nothing is typed `traffic_camera` unless its target literally says so (PKG-07 AC).
3. **Export column.** The sites export (`sites.csv` and its siblings per the release record shape) carries the `technology` column populated from the claim/vocab surface — F-329's missing field.
4. **Dossier/map facets.** The dossier and map surfaces gain a technology facet over the new field — additive, filterable, no-JS-compatible per the page-type rules (facets render server-side).
5. **Class authority.** `device_class` derives from the declared technology, not from source-id/operator substrings; the substring rules stay only as a compatibility assertion, and a mismatch between a target's declared slug and the old substring classification is logged as a finding, not silently resolved.
6. **Fixture coverage.** Fixture registry targets covering `alpr-fixed`, `camera-fixed-cctv`/CCTV, `camera-ptz` and a `traffic_camera`-declaring target assert each emits its declared technology, the export carries the column, and no non-traffic record claims `traffic_camera`. A target whose true class is automated traffic enforcement is **not** squeezed into a wrong slug — the ATE family lands with P36.3 (row 276, next in chain order); such a target either waits for it or carries a coarser declared level per SIG-ONTO-021, recorded in the run ledger.
7. **Requirement stamps** in the PR body: F-329, F-523 (as owned findings) + the cited ids above.
