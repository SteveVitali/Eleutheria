## Goal
The contributor system end to end: the five write tiers, onboarding, and safety. Contributions
enter at **L0 as evidence**, never directly at L1; no contributor PII is retained beyond the
documented window; pseudonymous contribution works at every tier; every contribution is revertible
as a unit (the revert is a new assertion); and vandalism/poisoning — including false-*absence*
campaigns — is resisted.

## In scope — deliverables
1. The **five-tier write model** (Anonymous, Registered, Trusted reviewer, Curator, Maintainer) with per-tier write scope and review requirement — SIG-CONTRIB-001. No tier may write a claim without provenance; submissions enter at **L0** as evidence, never directly at L1 — SIG-CONTRIB-002.
2. **Onboarding** path (pick a nearby open task, or submit an observation with a photo + location); the moderated usability-study protocol harness (median ≤10 min, publishable) — SIG-CONTRIB-003.
3. **Safety controls**: no retention of precise contributor geolocation beyond the submitted observation, real names, device identifiers, or IP logs beyond a short operational window — SIG-CONTRIB-005; know-your-rights + detention/harassment policy surfaces — SIG-CONTRIB-007/008.
4. **Pseudonymous contribution** fully supported at every tier including trusted-reviewer — SIG-CONTRIB-006.
5. **Revert as a unit**: every contribution revertible atomically, recorded as a new assertion (never a deletion) — SIG-CONTRIB-009.
6. **Vandalism/poisoning resistance**: anomaly detection routing bursts/coordinated/contested-resolving submissions to review not auto-reject — SIG-CONTRIB-010; equal guarding of false-*absence* campaigns — SIG-CONTRIB-011; the vendor operating-territory plausibility rule — SIG-CONTRIB-011a; the no-mass-revert / visual-weight disciplines — SIG-CONTRIB-011b/011c.

## Notes
This ticket **owns** the contributor-tier model, the retention-minimization posture ("what is not
stored cannot be subpoenaed"), and the revert-as-new-assertion contract that P16.2's contribution-back
depends on. The dominant live failure mode in this domain is **inflationary** — panic-driven or
adversarial over-reporting (R12-F12.28) — so the anomaly rules must not assume honest error. The
`Person`-creation constraints and officer test are enforced upstream (§11.3, §43.4); no contributor
tier below Curator may create a `Person`.
