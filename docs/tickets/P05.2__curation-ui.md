## Goal
The internal curation UI and review queue where humans adjudicate `PROPOSED` matches and
model-assisted extractions. LLM output reaches **only** the review queue — never the graph — with
`model_id` and `prompt_version` logged against each decision, and every model-extracted claim
carrying the source span that makes hallucination mechanically detectable.

## Load
- `docs/2_canonical_design_spec.md` §25 (LLM usage policy: permitted/prohibited uses, required scaffolding — span, model/prompt logging, `PROPOSED`/R6, graceful degradation), §27 (the ER pipeline stage and its review path).
- Part 0, Part I §3, Appendix E glossary.

## In scope — deliverables
1. The **internal curation UI + review queue**: a human accepts/rejects each tier-4/5 `PROPOSED` match, with the confidence explanation — the per-comparison match-weight decomposition from P05.1 — surfaced inline (SIG-IDENT-025).
2. **LLMs write nothing to the graph.** Models may generate review rationales / candidate extractions that land **only** in the queue; `model_id` and `prompt_version` are logged with each human decision — SIG-IDENT-026, SIG-LLM-001/002.
3. **Model-assisted extraction scaffolding**: every extraction records `model_id`, `prompt_version`, and the deterministic parameters used, and validates output against a schema — SIG-LLM-003.
4. **Source-span guardrail**: every model-extracted claim carries the exact source span; a span-less extraction is rejected; model-extracted claims are `R6` and enter as `PROPOSED` — SIG-LLM-004/005.
5. **Graceful degradation**: when the model is unavailable, work queues rather than fails, and no claim is emitted at a lowered evidentiary standard to compensate — SIG-LLM-007; a per-extraction-type review sampling rate with gold-set accuracy measurement and demotion-to-human-only on breach — SIG-LLM-006.
