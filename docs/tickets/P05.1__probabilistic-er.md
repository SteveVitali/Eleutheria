## Goal
The probabilistic top of the resolution cascade: a Splink-based matcher whose tiers 4–5 create
`PROPOSED` claims routed to review (never auto-write), sized blocking, a gold set with double
adjudication and a frozen holdout, holdout-driven auto-write demotion, and cluster-shape alerts —
the §14.7 quality gates that everything downstream depends on.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **Splink 4 on DuckDB** as the probabilistic matcher (AGPL/proprietary matchers excluded) — SIG-IDENT-021; ER as a distinct pipeline stage between `normalize()` and `load()` with its own run record, quality report, and rollback — SIG-RECON-001, and re-runnable over historical claims without destroying prior clustering — SIG-RECON-002.
2. **Tiers 4 (probabilistic) and 5 (weak-signal) create `PROPOSED` claims and enqueue review**; tier 6 persists no per-pair record — SIG-IDENT-020.
3. **Sized blocking**: blocking rules sized before use and rejected above a documented comparison ceiling; suffix-alone / state-alone blocking prohibited — SIG-IDENT-023; trigram similarity may power candidate search but never a decision score — SIG-IDENT-024.
4. **Match evidence**: every match records `match_tier`, `match_evidence`, and for probabilistic matches the match weight **and its per-comparison decomposition** — SIG-IDENT-025.
5. **Gold set**: stratified blocked-pair sampling across weight bands, double adjudication reporting Cohen's κ, a three-value label vocabulary, written rules, and a **frozen holdout**, versioned with per-label provenance — SIG-IDENT-027.
6. **Quality gates**: pairwise P/R/F1 at each tier boundary + B-cubed cluster P/R on the holdout; **auto-write tiers demoted to review when holdout precision falls below threshold** — SIG-IDENT-028; **cluster-shape alerts** on implausible clusters — SIG-IDENT-029; public identifiers stay stable across split/merge — SIG-IDENT-032.
