## Goal

Stop ingesting wrong geometries and correct the ones already in. Three halves: (a) **declaration** — each camera-registry target carries an explicit `axis_order` (lon-lat vs lat-lon) instead of an assumed default; (b) **detection** — ingest-time detectors flag axis-swap (a point outside the target's declared bbox/registry extent), null-island ((0,0) or out-of-range), and sign-flip candidates, emitting `geometry_defect` claims or marking the capture rather than silently ingesting wrong geometry; (c) **correction** — the F-322-identified rows get append-only correction claims on the hosted spine (the seven named defects: Nottingham/mark43 axis swaps, `trafficops` CA→FL, `gmh_emc` GA→Mongolia, `bangla` BD→Singapore, `oosgis` NL→Belgium, `dot_511_ga` (0,0) null-island), and portal-file `row_count` reports distinct entities or is disclosed — never an inflated count.

## In scope (deliverables)

1. **Per-target `axis_order`** — a declared field on every camera-registry target; ingest reads it; a missing field uses the documented default with a recorded note.
2. **Ingest detectors** — axis-swap (point outside the target's declared bbox/registry extent), null-island ((0,0) or out-of-range coordinates), sign-flip (hemisphere-impossible sign); each detector emits a `geometry_defect` claim or marks the capture — flagged, never silently corrected.
3. **Correction claims (hosted, OM-20-listed)** — append-only claims marking the seven F-322 defects (and any further rows the detectors identify), each carrying the defect class, the evidence and the corrected value as a new claim.
4. **Portal `row_count` truth** — the manifest reports distinct entities, or both `rows` and `distinct_entities` where they differ, disclosed.
5. **Re-ingest path** — a defect-flagged target re-ingests with its corrected axis order under the next normal ingest (the detector proves the fix).
6. Tests under `tests/`: each detector fires on its fixture (swapped point outside bbox → flagged; (0,0) → flagged; sign-impossible → flagged); a defect-free ingest is unmarked; `axis_order` per-target read respected.
