## Goal

Land the **probe-only** Flock transparency-portal connector under WV-10 (ADR-188): a single gentle, rate-limited probe request per known portal per run under the project UA (P35.38a), further page requests only while the portal keeps serving without a challenge, and **any** bot challenge, 403 or interstitial ends that portal's attempt and is recorded as a refusal with its kind. The probe set is the portals already known from the Eyes on Flock aggregator's inventory (I3: ≈ 917) — this ADR adds no discovery method. A page served without a challenge is parsed with the mirror's portal schema, reconciled against the mirror, Part VIII-screened **in memory before persistence** (ADR-185), its terms captured verbatim, and lands in the **CC BY-SA 4.0 compartment** — never merged into the CC-BY graph. Expected yield today ≈ 0 (F2.1); the Eyes on Flock aggregator remains the portal-layer source (the aggregator-source clause of SIG-INGEST-035 stands — only the no-direct-capture clause is waived). The new registry row lands `ingestion_permitted=false`; activation is the operator's ING-GO-B execution at P36.12.
