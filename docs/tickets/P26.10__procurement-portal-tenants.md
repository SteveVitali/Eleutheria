## Goal
Procurement is where surveillance capability is *bought* — RFPs and awards for
ALPR, drones, RTCC platforms, gunshot detection, body-worn cameras are the
strongest predictive signal in the graph. The vendor portals are multi-tenant
exactly like the agenda platforms: **Periscope S2G / BidNet Direct** (hundreds of
agency storefronts), **Bonfire**, **OpenGov Procurement**, plus city-open-data
contract registers (Socrata award datasets). One connector × N tenants again.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **Enumeration.** Tenant/host lists per platform: BidNet/Periscope agency
   storefronts (`*.bidnetdirect.com`), Bonfire portals, OpenGov portals, plus
   city Socrata contract/award datasets (many `data.<city>.gov` procurement
   layers). Record the enumeration source per host; targets as data.
2. **Surveillance-relevance.** Reuse the P26.6 reviewed keyword vocab
   (ALPR/flock/rtcc/drone/shotspotter/body-worn/cctv/facial-recognition +
   procurement-matter types) against RFP/award titles+descriptions; emit
   `procurement_notice` claims with document locators + verbatim `raw_value`
   — never inferred capability, only what the record says.
3. **Claims + wiring.** Extend the `procurement` connector's tenant pattern
   (it already owns cooperative-vehicle + agenda platforms); bounded windows
   per tenant; fail-closed on drift; idempotent dedupe.
4. **Hosted ingest.** `sig-ingest-*` jobs + monthly schedulers for yielding
   platforms; per-host outcomes recorded; spine delta measured.
5. **Records.** live_runs/ + GCS run rows; gated/keyed portals become
   DEFERRALS rows naming their blocker; ladder re-measured.

## Out of scope
- Login-walled portals (recorded, never bypassed); non-surveillance
  procurement breadth (vocab stays surveillance-scoped); sam_gov/openstates
  (already keyed, P26.4); flipping unresolved terms.

- [ ] jobs + schedulers for yielding platforms; idempotent re-run proven *(live-gated)*
