# P12.2 — Access edges and access-path closure

- **Sequence:** 30 of 46
- **Phase:** 12 — Usage and network layer
- **base_branch:** current checkout — the branch the previous ticket left checked out (stacked PR chain); `main` for P00.1
- **Depends on (must land earlier in the stack):** P12.1 (the usage/analytics layer; `observed_use` aggregates and configured edges that this inference reads)

## Goal
Model the three access edge types so they are **never merged**, and implement access-path closure —
SIG's most powerful and most dangerous inference — bounded by hop limits, scope, and non-composition
rules, with paths beyond a published hop threshold labelled **speculative** and excluded from
headline figures.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **The three sharing edge types**, distinct and **never merged, collapsed, or defaulted into one another**: `configured_access` (set up to permit), `observed_use` (someone actually did it), `declared_policy` (someone said it is permitted/forbidden); their disagreement surfaced as a finding; single-snapshot edges carry `valid_from_kind='unknown'`; an `observed_use` edge never creates/implies a `configured_access` edge or vice versa (the L4 inference is labelled, not an L1 edge; the non-implication rule SIG-RECON-037 is owned/tested in P08.2 and enforced here at the edge model) — SIG-ONTO-042/043/044.
2. **`AccessRelationship`** with **required** `direction` (never symmetric by default), `scope`, `automaticity`, `access_kind` (which of the three edge types), and `asserted_by` (enabling asymmetry detection) — SIG-ONTO-049.
3. **Access-path closure** — "can organization A reach organization B's data, through any chain?" — implemented at L4 with `derivation_rule`/`derived_at`/`input_claim_ids`, and **bounded**: only `configured_access` and `federates_search_to` compose; `observed_use` does not compose; `distributes_list_to` does not compose in the query direction; scope must be respected (a partner-scoped edge does not chain into a national-scoped one); every hop must be currently valid at the as-of time (a path through an expired edge is historical and labelled so); path length capped and reported with the full hop list and each hop's evidence; confidence is the **minimum over the path**, never the average — SIG-RECON-048/049.
4. **Speculative labelling** — beyond a published hop count, closure output is labelled **speculative** and **excluded from headline figures**; every published path shows its full hop list with per-hop evidence (no unexplained "A can reach B") — SIG-RECON-050.

## Notes
This ticket **owns** the three-edge-type separation and the access-path-closure bound. The
composition rules (SIG-RECON-049) are the safety mechanism: `observed_use` composing would turn "A
searched B and B searched C" into a false "A can search C", and an unlabelled long path turns a
finding into an insinuation. The difference between "these two agencies share data" and "a seven-hop
theoretical path exists" MUST survive into every surface — including when the blurred version would
be more striking (SIG-RECON-050).
