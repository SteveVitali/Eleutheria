## Goal

The a-half of the PKG-10 public-number corrections: **every surface's provenance and coverage numbers derive from that surface's own evidence set, and coverage ratios stop reading N of N while records are withheld.** (1) Per-dossier provenance — the export emits per-dossier (and per-surface) provenance rows so `BaseLayout`'s `provenance ?? getSiteProvenance()` fallback dies for dossiers: each dossier's "How we know this" derives from *its own* sources/claims/evidence rows, and a surface with no emitted provenance renders "site-wide" explicitly rather than silently presenting site totals as its own (ED-41). (2) Unclamped coverage — the coverage numerator counts subjects with a resolved, publishable, non-conflicted value (a tier-0/currency-filtered count), the `min(resolved, claimed)` clamp is dropped, and the excluded counts (conflicted, withheld, filtered) are disclosed — the F-134 defect ("232,625 of 232,625 subjects with a camera_latitude claim" while 5,290 are withheld as conflicting) cannot read true again (ED-42). The catalog AC: C3's number-trace rows for these surfaces re-derive exactly — the trace is the oracle; the displays are corrected to match it, never the trace edited to match the displays.
