## Goal
The second real connector — of deliberately different shape from `osm` — ingesting the EFF Atlas of
Surveillance agency-adoption feed. It writes `deployment_exists` at family-level granularity while
preserving upstream source attribution and the Atlas vocabulary version, and records a retired
Atlas category as a **retirement, not a world change**.

## Load

- Part 0, Part I §3, Appendix E glossary.

## In scope — deliverables
1. The `atlas` connector on the P04.1 interface, writing `deployment_exists` at **family-level technology granularity**, with Atlas's own source attribution preserved on each row.
2. **Keying on the Atlas agency identifier**, routing non-ORI-shaped values to the surrogate identity path (feeding P03.2's crosswalk), never resolving entities itself (SIG-INGEST-034).
3. **Predicate allowlist** enforced: MUST NOT write device counts, coordinates, configuration, or current status — only adoption-level `deployment_exists`.
4. **Evidence-genre preservation**: where the upstream records which of its nine methodology components produced a row (OSINT, news, gov docs, minutes, press releases, procurement leads, crowdsourcing, staff review, imported datasets), carry it; where it does not, record the granularity loss rather than guess a tier.
5. **Supersession + retirement**: later evidence may supersede or temporally qualify an Atlas row, and a retired Atlas category is recorded as a category retirement, so a vocabulary disappearance is never read as a world change — SIG-ONTO-059; the Atlas vocabulary version is recorded on every row.
6. Committed fixtures + a canary.
