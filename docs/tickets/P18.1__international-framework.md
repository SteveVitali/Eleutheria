## Goal
The jurisdiction adapter framework: an adapter checklist satisfiable with **no US-shaped
assumption**; organization and legal-instrument types added under a **national namespace** (not by
widening a US enum); multilingual labels with **BCP 47** tags; **jurisdiction-conditional
publication** (§43.8); and coarse international datasets ingested with **explicit coarse
granularity**, never disaggregated to agency level by inference.

## In scope — deliverables
1. The **jurisdiction adapter checklist** — a reusable framework a new country plugs into with no US-shaped assumption (jurisdiction code system, org types, legal instruments, records-request vocabulary, publication rules) — SIG-CHART-029/030.
2. **Organization and legal-instrument types under a national namespace** (`fr.*`, `uk.*`, `de.*`, …) with a shared abstract parent per concept, added **without widening any US enum** — SIG-ONTO-068.
3. **Multilingual labels** on every label-bearing term and entity name, repeatable and tagged with **BCP 47** (transliterations carry a `transliteration_scheme` qualifier) — SIG-ONTO-069.
4. **Jurisdiction-conditional publication**: the policy engine evaluates the jurisdiction of the data subject and of the record's origin (a single global rule is unavailable) — SIG-PUB-017.
5. **Coarse international datasets** (country-level indices, global FR maps, vendor-level international datasets) ingested as claims with **explicit coarse granularity** and **never disaggregated to agency level by inference** — SIG-ENG-036 / SIG-INGEST-042.
