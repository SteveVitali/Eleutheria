## In scope — deliverables
1. **Procurement/renewal watch**: for every contract — expiry, renewal window, notice deadline, approving body, next scheduled meeting, and replacement procurement if known — SIG-UI-026.
2. **Subscriptions by jurisdiction** with **iCal and RSS** output, keyed on `next_decision_date`, so a local group can drop a renewal deadline into their own calendar — SIG-UI-027.
3. **Evidence recommender** producing, for an upcoming decision point, a ranked list of the most useful evidence artifacts, using only the model inputs of §39.5a (directness `D`, currency vs volatility, open contradictions, open tasks, artifact-type-vs-decision, capture_status) — SIG-UI-027a (SIG-CHART-008 / journey J-3).
4. **Neutrality guard**: the recommender **MUST NOT rank by persuasiveness, sentiment, or predicted vote effect** — directness, recency, and dispute status only — SIG-UI-027b.
5. **Exportable citation list** with permalinks and as-of dates, suitable for attaching to public comment — SIG-UI-027c.
6. **Evidence viewer**: renders the document with the supporting span highlighted at its locator, showing the claim; extraction method + version; review status; conflicting claims; capture date + digest; acquisition method; and the full claim history — SIG-UI-028.
7. **Capture diffing**: diff two captures of the same artifact field by field ("what changed between June and August") — SIG-UI-029 (§29.7).
8. **Sealed captures**: metadata-only representation with an explanation of why the bytes are withheld — SIG-UI-030 (§17.5).
