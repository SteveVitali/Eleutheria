## In scope — deliverables
1. **Research queue** surface: task cards stating the closing condition, the evidence sought, the assignee class, and the effort estimate; geographic filtering; claiming with expiry; the full disposition vocabulary incl. "searched, found nothing" — SIG-UI-031 (§33.2).
2. **Public corrections log** as a first-class page, listing every correction with what changed, when, why, and who reported it — the required eighth surface — SIG-UI-032.
3. **Dispute/correction submission path** on every page, one click from any claim — SIG-UI-033 (§45).
4. **Methodology, data-freshness (§32.4), and coverage-metrics pages**, public and linked from every dossier — SIG-UI-034.
5. **Editorial standards conformance**:
   - the recorded **hostile-reader review** per dossier template version, committed alongside the version, release blocked until every finding is dispositioned — SIG-UI-042;
   - the six **register rules** enforced — SIG-UI-043;
   - the **"How we know this" module** on every page (artifact counts, tier distribution, source-independence count, date range, rules applied, human-review status) — SIG-UI-044;
   - the **three example editorial cases** (pending lawsuit; policy/configuration divergence; cancellation with hardware remaining) rendered as specified — SIG-UI-045;
   - a public **style guide** codifying the rules, applied to generated rationale templates as well as hand-written copy — SIG-UI-046.
6. **Seven-surfaces-plus-corrections-log** cross-surface assertion: verify all seven outline surfaces (dossier, map, network, watch, evidence recommender, evidence viewer, research queue) plus the corrections log exist across Phase 15.
